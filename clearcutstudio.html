Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens inside your browser. The AI model is downloaded once from a public CDN and then runs on your device using WebAssembly.

Why is the first background removal slow?

The AI model is a large download on first use (tens of megabytes). It needs an internet connection and can take a minute on slow networks.

Why are big photos shrunk to 1200 px for background removal?

Very large images can exhaust memory on phones. Scaling to a maximum of 1200 px keeps the AI step reliable on mobile; the Resize & Compress tool works at full size.

What if the AI result is not perfect?

Use the manual colour eraser: tap the background colour and adjust the tolerance. It works best on plain backgrounds.
