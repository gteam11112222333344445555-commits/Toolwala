Frequently asked questions

What resolution are the wallpapers?

Downloads are 1080 × 1920 pixels (portrait), which fits most modern phones.

Where do the photos come from?

Photos are served by Picsum Photos (Lorem Picsum), a free photo placeholder service built on Unsplash photographers' work. They are free for personal wallpaper use.

Where is the downloaded wallpaper saved?

It goes to your device's Downloads folder or Files app. On iPhone, open it and choose Save Image to add it to Photos.

What do Minimal, Dark and Soft mean?

Minimal is black and white, Dark applies a darker mood grade, and Soft adds a gentle blur – ideal behind icons and widgets.
