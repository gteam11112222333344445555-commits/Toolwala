Frequently asked questions

Is my invoice data uploaded?

No. The invoice is built and converted to PDF inside your browser; nothing is sent to a server.

Does the PDF download work on my phone?

Yes. On mobile the invoice is rendered in a hidden off-screen layer so the PDF is always complete, even if the Preview tab is not open.

Can I change the currency?

Yes. Choose $, Rs, €, £, ₹ or AED from the currency list.

How are tax and discount calculated?

The discount is taken off the subtotal first, and tax is then applied to the discounted amount.
