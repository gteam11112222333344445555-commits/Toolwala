Frequently asked questions

How is WPM calculated?

Words per minute counts every 5 correct characters as one word, divided by the minutes elapsed. Mistakes reduce your accuracy.

When does the timer start?

The 60-second timer starts automatically on your first keystroke.

Are generated passwords stored or sent anywhere?

No. Passwords are created in your browser with the Web Crypto API (crypto.getRandomValues) and are never uploaded.

What is a good typing speed?

Around 40 WPM is average, 60–80 WPM is fast and above 80 WPM is professional level.
