Frequently asked questions

Are all of these AI tools free?

Most have a free plan with limits (Freemium); a few are free to use. Pricing and limits change often, so always confirm on the tool's own website.

How do I use the prompts?

Press Copy Prompt, paste it into ChatGPT, Claude or Midjourney and replace the [BRACKETED] parts with your own details.

Does AIFinderHub get paid for listing tools?

No. Listings are independent and not sponsored. Each link opens the tool's official website in a new tab.

Is my search stored?

No. Searching and filtering happen in your browser only.
