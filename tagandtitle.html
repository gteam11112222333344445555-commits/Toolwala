Frequently asked questions

How do I write a high-CTR title?

Lead with curiosity or a clear benefit, add a number, keep it near 60 characters and put the most important words first.

What is a 3-second hook?

The very first line of your video. It should create a question, a surprise or a promise so viewers do not swipe away.

Do stylish fonts work in PUBG and Free Fire?

Most Unicode styles work, but some games block certain symbols. If a style is rejected, try another one.

Are hashtags guaranteed to go viral?

No tool can guarantee that. Use the hashtags as a starting point and mix broad and niche tags.
