Frequently asked questions

Where do the exchange rates come from?

Rates are loaded from free public currency APIs that need no key. If the first source is unavailable the next one is tried, and offline estimates are used as a last resort.

Are the rates the same as my bank's?

No. These are mid-market reference rates. Banks and money-transfer services add their own margin, so always check the final rate before sending money.

How is exact age calculated?

The calculator counts full years, then months, then days between the birth date and the chosen date, handling different month lengths correctly.

How do I calculate a discount?

Choose Discount, enter the percentage and the original price. You will see the amount saved and the final price.
